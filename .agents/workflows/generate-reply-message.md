# Generate Reply Message
This workflow generates a structured reply message to stakeholders, QA, or team members. It ensures the tone is collaborative and focused on solutions while retaining necessary technical context.

## Steps
1. **Determine Scope**: Analyze the user's issue, request, or complaint to understand the core concern and the technical context provided.
2. **Message Generation**: Generate a comprehensive message based on the following exact structure:
   - **Acknowledge and Validate**: Start by directly validating the issue/request. Acknowledge the frustration or the importance of their concern without being defensive.
   - **Technical Context (Brief)**: Provide a concise, excellent technical explanation of *why* the issue occurred or the constraints involved. Keep it factual and avoid sounding like an excuse.
   - **Actionable Takeaways**: Explicitly list what you are going to do differently (or what steps are being taken) to answer/resolve/fix/debug the issue or request. This addresses the core concern moving forward.
   - **Collaborative Tone**: Praise the reportee, QA, or stakeholder for catching the issue or raising the concern, rather than just explaining your own process. This fosters a better team dynamic and shows appreciation for their attention to detail.
3. **Review & Refine**: Ensure the final message avoids overly defensive language and pivots strongly toward addressing the core issue.
